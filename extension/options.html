Study Buddy Exam Mode

For grown-ups: connect this extension to your Study Buddy server.

How exam mode works

  • A grown-up turns exam mode on in the Study Buddy parent page. Within 30 seconds the extension shows a red EXAM badge.
  • Sites on the parent's list are paused and show a friendly "paused for your exam" page.
  • It counts paused-site visits, tab switches and times Chrome loses focus. Numbers only: no page content, no list of websites, no screenshots.
  • When the exam ends, everything unpauses automatically, even if the server can't be reached.
